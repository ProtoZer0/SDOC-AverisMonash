Direction A

Facing Pages

The argument: evidence is a place. The instruction and the draft are laid open side by side as the two real documents, every line of them. The seven lines we compare are named in the margin; every other line is only numbered, so the page itself answers "which fields do you check". Seven hairline threads stitch each named line to its twin. Click one and the thread draws itself, both values sweep, and there is no separate evidence panel to open, because the evidence is already on the table. A refusal is not an empty page: the draft's slot holds a record of exactly what could not be read, and every thread from the instruction stops at its edge.

What it costs. Two full documents need the vertical room, so the margin notes shrink to one strip and the per-field details move under the pages. The thread is geometry: it must be recomputed on resize and after fonts load, and it is dropped below 1160px. When scanned PDFs arrive, this layout needs a page-image renderer to keep its promise.
